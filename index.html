About The Company

A specialist insurer backed by one of the world's largest insurance groups

Tokio Marine Kiln is a leading international specialty insurer with a reputation for underwriting excellence, outstanding claims service and innovative products. Formed in 2014 through the merger of R.J. Kiln, a Lloyd's syndicate founded in 1963, and Tokio Marine Europe, it operates as a Lloyd's managing agency alongside its own insurance company platform.

TMK is part of the Tokio Marine Group, one of the world's oldest and largest insurance groups, founded in Japan in 1879 and now operating across the globe. That backing gives TMK the stability and reach of a major international group, combined with the specialist, close-knit culture of a Lloyd's market underwriter.

The Cyber Centre of Excellence sits at the heart of how the Group approaches cyber risk, and this role gives you a genuinely global stage from which to influence it.

The Opportunity

Bring your threat intelligence expertise to a global cyber underwriting team

Tokio Marine Holdings (TMHD) drives high quality, risk-adequate underwriting and accumulation control across the Group's cyber lines of business. As part of a cross-line unit of cyber insurance experts, you will support the Global Head of Cyber Risk in developing the Group's cyber underwriting strategy, risk appetite, risk limits, underwriting guidelines and accumulation control tools, covering both affirmative and non-affirmative cyber coverages.

You will be employed by Tokio Marine Holdings and seconded to work day to day within Tokio Marine Kiln's Cyber Centre of Excellence, based at their London office. It's a highly visible role at the centre of how a major international insurer thinks about cyber threat intelligence, giving you the platform to build market-facing thought leadership, shape internal tooling decisions, and work alongside specialists across the Tokio Marine Cyber Community.

You'll split your time between deep analytical work, such as authoring the Monthly Digest and contributing to the bi-annual CCoE Horizon Scan, and more people-facing activity: delivering technical training through the Tokio Marine Cyber Academy and building relationships with cyber experts across the Group.

What You'll Be Doing

A blend of intelligence, strategy and stakeholder impact

Core responsibilities

  • Monitor key cyber threats and the broader cybersecurity landscape, maintaining a repository of data sources with the Senior Cyber Risk Specialist.
  • Lead the gathering and analysis of insurance market intelligence, notably authoring the Monthly Digest report.
  • Contribute to the bi-annual CCoE Horizon Scan and regular cyber market intelligence reporting.
  • Support delivery of cyber technical training through the Tokio Marine Cyber Academy.
  • Support the production of Realistic Disaster Scenarios and broader risk parametrisation for the internal accumulation model.
  • Lead the CCoE's assessment and selection of technical tools, and develop external scanning reports tracking single points of failure.

Where you'll add value

  • Develop your cyber insurance knowledge to support delivery of the cyber security strategy and Mid-term Plan.
  • Build relationships across the Tokio Marine Cyber Community, including the Cyber Threat Unit and the Cyber Special Interest Group.
  • Produce external communications and thought leadership content that positions Tokio Marine as a cyber authority.
  • Manage stakeholders with appropriate, regular communication for each audience.
  • Contribute articles and help maintain the CCoE Portal.
  • Continue developing subject matter knowledge, providing insight and support to the wider CCoE team.
What We're Looking For

The experience and mindset that will help you thrive

Technical requirements

  • University degree in a relevant field.
  • Practical experience in cyber risk and risk assessment.
  • Ability to collect, analyse and disseminate threat intelligence, both actionable and strategic.

  • Genuine interest in cyber threats and risk, with a drive to keep learning through industry events.

  • Strong Microsoft Office skills, able to produce reports for senior management; Adobe suite experience is a plus.
